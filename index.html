About Rocket League

Rocket League is a high-powered hybrid of arcade-style soccer and vehicular mayhem. Players compete in teams to score goals by driving rocket-powered cars in fast-paced matches. The game has become a global sensation, known for its competitive gameplay and unique mechanics.

Key Features

Competitive Modes

Challenge players worldwide in ranked matches, tournaments, and casual play modes.

Customization

Personalize your vehicles with a wide range of decals, wheels, and rocket boosts.

Cross-Platform Play

Enjoy playing matches with friends across all major gaming platforms (PC, PlayStation, Nintendo, Xbox)
