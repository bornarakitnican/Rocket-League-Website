Rocket League Sideswipe

Rocket League has a mobile version and it is called Sideswipe.

It is available for free on any smart device.

The game in size is 2GB+

How to play

Sideswipe is basically Rocket League in 2D.

Your aim is to score more goals than your opponent in 2 minutes apose to 5 minutes in regular Rocket League.
